      {# The rest of the differences, which the demo above cannot show. #}
      Comes from
      getElementsByClassName, getElementsByTagName, .children, document.forms give an HTMLCollection. querySelectorAll and .childNodes give a NodeList.
      Live or static
      Every HTMLCollection is live. A NodeList can be either: querySelectorAll is a snapshot, .childNodes is live. So "NodeList" does not mean "static".
      That is why .children and .childNodes are not the same thing twice. .children skips text and comments; .childNodes keeps them, so the whitespace between two tags in your HTML counts as a node.
      Holds
      An HTMLCollection holds elements only. A NodeList holds any node, including text and comment nodes.
      Looping
      A NodeList has forEach. An HTMLCollection does not. Both work with for...of and with a plain index, and neither has map or filter: for those, convert first with Array.from(list) or [...list].
      Lookup by name
      An HTMLCollection can be reached by id or name as well as by number, so document.forms["profile"] works. A NodeList is numbers only.
